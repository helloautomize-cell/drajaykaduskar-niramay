**H1:** Coming from outside Nagpur?

Half our patients travel to us from towns and villages around Nagpur. This page helps you plan the trip so your consultation, [tests](/lab/) and medicines are finished in one visit.

#### Planning your day
- **Book ahead** by phone or WhatsApp so we can [plan your visit](/plan-your-visit/).
- **If you need a fasting blood test,** arrive by 7 am when the laboratory opens, do not eat before the sample, and carry your [diabetes](/diabetes/) medicines to take with breakfast after the test.
- The laboratory is open Monday to Saturday, 7 am to 7 pm; consultations are 8:30 am to 6 pm.
- Most routine reports are ready the same day, so in most cases you can discuss results with the doctor before you travel back.

#### One visit, everything in one place
- Consultation, blood and urine tests, ECG, 2D Echo, treadmill test and eye screening are all done at the clinic.
- Pick up your medicines at our [pharmacy](/pharmacy/) right after your consultation, before you leave.

#### Follow-up without travel
Where clinically appropriate, your doctor may review stable follow-up reports by phone or WhatsApp, so you do not always need to travel for a routine review.

#### Getting here
Niramay Clinics is at 572, Indu Bhaskar Apartments, Dr. N. B. Khare Marg, opposite Dinanath High School, Dhantoli, about 3 km from Nagpur railway station and central bus stands. Two-wheeler parking is available at the building.

Patients regularly visit us from Kamptee, Hingna, Butibori, Kalmeshwar, Saoner, Katol, Narkhed, Umred, Bhiwapur, Kuhi, Mauda, Ramtek, Parseoni, Bhandara, Tumsar, Wardha, Hinganghat and Pandhurna.

#### Frequently asked questions

**Can consultation, tests and medicines be done in one trip?**
Usually yes. If you share your reports and requirements in advance, we plan the consultation, lab work and pharmacy so that most patients finish in a single day.

**Should I bring my previous reports?**
Yes, bring previous prescriptions, recent lab reports, discharge summaries and your medicine list. They help the doctor avoid repeating tests and understand your history quickly.

**Do I need to come fasting?**
Come fasting only if a fasting test is planned; we will tell you when you book. Fasting usually means no food for 8 to 12 hours, with water and regular medicines allowed unless advised.

**Which towns do patients usually come from?**
Patients visit us from across Nagpur and from towns up to about 100 km away, including Wardha, Bhandara, Umred, Katol, Saoner and Ramtek.

**How far in advance should I book?**
A few days' notice is enough for most visits. Booking early helps us schedule your consultation, tests and pharmacy so everything fits into one trip.
