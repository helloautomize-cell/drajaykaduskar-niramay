**Quick answer.** Insulin resistance, excess body fat, high sugar intake, refined carbohydrates, alcohol and uncontrolled diabetes can all raise triglycerides. Very high levels can cause pancreatitis, and moderately high levels raise heart risk. Weight loss, diet changes, activity, good diabetes control and sometimes medicines bring them down.

Triglycerides are a type of fat in the blood. High levels often go hand in hand with diabetes and excess weight.

#### Why Triglycerides Rise

- Insulin resistance increases fat production in the liver

- Excess calories, especially from sugar and refined carbohydrates

- Alcohol

- Poorly controlled diabetes

- Certain medicines and conditions

- Genetic tendencies

#### Why It Matters

- Higher risk of heart disease

- Often accompanies low HDL and fatty liver

- Very high levels can trigger acute pancreatitis

#### How to Lower Triglycerides

##### Diet

- Reduce sweets, sugary drinks and refined flour

- Limit fried and processed foods

- Choose fish, nuts, vegetables and whole grains

- Limit or avoid alcohol

##### Lifestyle

- Lose weight

- Exercise regularly

##### Medical

- Better blood sugar control

- Medicines when levels remain high

#### Testing

A fasting blood test measures triglycerides along with cholesterol. Your doctor will advise how often to repeat it.

#### Key Takeaways

- Diabetes and obesity raise triglycerides.

- Sugar, refined carbs and alcohol worsen them.

- Weight loss and diet changes help a lot.

- Control of diabetes matters.

- Very high levels need urgent care.

#### When to seek urgent care

Seek urgent care for chest pain, breathlessness, confusion, vomiting, or symptoms of very high or very low sugar.

#### Frequently asked questions

**What is a normal triglyceride level?**

Typically below 150 mg/dL, though your doctor will interpret it for you.

**Does eating fat raise triglycerides?**

Excess calories and sugar often matter more than dietary fat alone.

**Does alcohol raise triglycerides?**

Yes, even moderate drinking can raise them in some people.

**Can triglycerides be lowered quickly?**

Diet and activity can lower them within weeks.

**Do I need a fasting test?**

Your doctor will advise whether fasting is needed.

**Are triglycerides linked to fatty liver?**

Yes, they are closely related.
