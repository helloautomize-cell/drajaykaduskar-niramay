**Quick answer.** In clinical studies, people taking GLP-1 medicines for weight management lost on average about 5 to 15 percent of their body weight over several months to a year, depending on the medicine, dose and lifestyle. People with type 2 diabetes usually lose somewhat less. Results vary widely, and weight is often regained if treatment stops.

Social media is full of dramatic before-and-after photos. Real results are meaningful but more gradual, and they differ from person to person. Knowing what is realistic helps you set goals and avoid disappointment.

#### What Do Studies Show?

Large trials have shown that:

- Semaglutide at higher weight-management doses led to average weight loss in the range of about 15 percent over roughly a year and a half in people without diabetes.

- Tirzepatide at higher doses showed average losses of about 15 to 20 percent or more in some studies.

- In people with type 2 diabetes, average weight loss is usually lower, often around 5 to 10 percent.

- Lower doses used mainly for blood sugar control give smaller weight changes.

These are averages. Some people lose less, and a few lose more.

#### How Long Does It Take?

- **First 4 to 8 weeks:** The dose is gradually increased. Weight loss is often slow at first.

- **3 to 6 months:** Most visible weight loss begins as the dose approaches the target.

- **6 to 12 months:** Weight loss usually peaks, then levels off.

- **After a year:** Weight usually stabilises while treatment continues.

#### What Else Improves Besides Weight?

- Blood sugar and HbA1c

- Blood pressure

- Waist size and belly fat

- Fatty liver markers

- Cholesterol and triglycerides

- Sleep apnoea and joint pain in some people

- For some medicines, heart and kidney benefits in selected patients

#### What Affects Your Results?

- **The medicine and dose:** Different molecules and strengths produce different results.

- **Your starting weight:** People with higher body weight often lose more kilograms, although the percentage may be similar.

- **Diabetes status:** Weight loss is often smaller in people with diabetes.

- **Diet and activity:** Protein-rich meals, fibre and regular activity improve results and protect muscle.

- **Sleep and stress:** Poor sleep and high stress can slow progress.

- **Side effects:** Severe nausea may limit the dose.

- **Consistency:** Missing doses reduces results.

- **Other medicines:** Some medicines for diabetes, mood or epilepsy can cause weight gain.

#### Do You Lose Only Fat?

Some of the weight lost can be muscle. To protect it, eat enough protein, do strength exercises two to three times a week and avoid crash diets. A dietitian can help with a personalised plan.

#### What If You Do Not Lose Weight?

A small number of people respond poorly. Your doctor will check your dose, adherence, diet, sleep, other medicines and conditions such as thyroid disease or Cushing's syndrome. Sometimes a different medicine or approach is better.

#### How Should You Set Goals?

- Aim for gradual loss of around 0.5 to 1 kg a week at most, after the initial phase.

- Set the first goal at about 5 percent of body weight.

- Track waist size, energy and blood sugar, not just the scale.

- Review progress with your doctor every few weeks to months.

#### When Should You See a Doctor?

See your doctor if you lose weight very fast, feel weak or dizzy, have severe stomach pain, cannot eat or drink, or notice signs of dehydration.

#### Key Takeaways

- Average weight loss with GLP-1 medicines ranges from about 5 to 15 percent or more, depending on the medicine and dose.

- People with diabetes usually lose less.

- Most weight loss occurs over 6 to 12 months.

- Protein, strength training and sleep protect results and muscle.

- Treatment is a long-term plan, not a quick fix.

#### Frequently asked questions

**How much weight can I lose with semaglutide?**

At weight-management doses, studies show average loss of about 15 percent of body weight over around a year and a half, though individuals vary. In type 2 diabetes the average is lower. Your result depends on dose, diet and consistency.

**How much weight can I lose with tirzepatide?**

At higher doses, studies show average weight loss of about 15 to 20 percent or more in people without diabetes. In people with diabetes, it is usually less. Individual results vary.

**How fast will I lose weight on a GLP-1 medicine?**

Most people lose weight slowly at first, as the dose increases over weeks. Noticeable loss usually appears after a few months and peaks at about 6 to 12 months.

**Can I lose weight without exercise on GLP-1?**

You may, but it is not ideal. Without strength exercise and enough protein, more of the weight lost can be muscle. Activity also improves blood sugar, mood and long-term maintenance.

**Why am I not losing weight on a GLP-1 medicine?**

Possible reasons include a low dose, missed doses, high-calorie liquids, poor sleep, other medicines or medical conditions. Review your habits and dose with your doctor before changing anything.

**Will I regain weight after stopping?**

Many people regain a significant part of the weight when they stop, because appetite returns. A maintenance plan with your doctor helps reduce this risk.
