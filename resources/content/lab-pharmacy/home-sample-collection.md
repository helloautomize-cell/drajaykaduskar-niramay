**H1:** Home sample collection

For elderly patients, people with limited mobility, busy families, or anyone who prefers not to travel, our laboratory team can collect blood and urine samples at home.

#### How it works
1. **Book at least one day in advance** by phone or WhatsApp on +91 84591 41584.
2. Tell us the tests (or share the doctor's prescription), the patient's age and address, and whether the test needs fasting.
3. Our trained staff member visits at the agreed time with sealed, single-use collection material.
4. Samples are brought to Niramay Laboratory and processed.
5. Reports are shared with you and, if you wish, with your doctor.

#### Service area and charges
We collect samples at home anywhere in Nagpur city (within about 15 km of Dhantoli), from 7 am, Monday to Saturday, at no extra charge. Samples are tested in our own lab.

#### Tips
- For fasting tests, the collection will be early in the morning. Do not eat until the sample is taken.
- Keep the prescription, ID and previous reports ready.
- Keep the arm sleeve loose.

#### Frequently asked questions

**How do I book a home collection?**
Call or WhatsApp the clinic with your test list or prescription. We schedule a trained phlebotomist, confirm the time the previous day, and collect at your doorstep.

**Which areas do you cover?**
We cover Dhantoli and most of Nagpur city. For areas further out, call and we will confirm whether a visit is possible at your location.

**How will I receive my reports?**
Reports are delivered as soon as they are ready, usually the same day for routine tests, and are also available to your doctor if you consult at Niramay.

**Do I need to be fasting for a home visit?**
Only for tests that need fasting, such as fasting sugar or a fasting lipid profile. Tell us when you book; we schedule early-morning visits so your fast is not longer than needed.

**Can I book a collection for an elderly parent?**
Yes. Many bookings are for parents and patients who find travel difficult. Share the test list and a convenient time, and our phlebotomist will collect the sample at their doorstep.
