**H1:** Niramay Laboratory

Niramay Laboratory is on the premises of Niramay Clinics, so most tests can be done on the day of your consultation and reviewed by your doctor without another trip.

**Timings:** 7 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday. Sundays closed.
**Home sample collection:** available with one day's prior booking

#### Tests available
- **[Diabetes](/diabetes/):** fasting and post-meal blood sugar, HbA1c, glucose tolerance test, urine albumin to creatinine ratio
- **Heart and cholesterol:** lipid profile
- **Kidney:** creatinine, urea, eGFR, electrolytes, uric acid, urine routine
- **Liver:** liver function tests
- **Thyroid:** TSH, free T4, free T3
- **Blood:** complete blood count, ESR, iron studies
- **Vitamins:** vitamin D, vitamin B12
- **Pregnancy:** glucose tolerance test for gestational diabetes
- **Others** on request. All tests are processed in our own laboratory at the clinic.

#### Quality
- Automated biochemistry and haematology analysers
- Reports are checked and signed by a qualified pathologist

#### How to prepare for common tests
| Test | Preparation |
|---|---|
| Fasting blood sugar, lipid profile | 10 to 12 hours of fasting; water is allowed. Your doctor may say a non-fasting lipid test is acceptable. |
| Post-meal (PP) blood sugar | Sample exactly 2 hours after starting your usual breakfast or lunch. Note the time you started eating. |
| HbA1c, thyroid, CBC | No fasting needed |
| Glucose tolerance test | Arrive fasting; you will drink a glucose solution and stay at the clinic for about 2 hours |
| Urine tests | A mid-stream sample in the container we provide; first morning sample if advised |

Take your usual medicines unless your doctor has told you otherwise. If you take diabetes medicines or insulin, bring them and take them after the fasting sample, with your meal.

#### Reports
Most routine reports are ready the same day. Collect them at the clinic or ask us to send them to you. Please discuss abnormal results with your doctor rather than starting or stopping treatment on your own.

#### Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an appointment for lab tests?**
Walk-ins are welcome during lab hours (Monday to Saturday, 7 am to 7 pm). Fasting tests are easiest early in the morning. Home collection needs prior booking.

**How do I get my reports?**
Reports are shared once ready, usually the same day for routine tests. They can be collected from the clinic or sent to you, and they reach your Niramay doctor directly if your consultation is with us.

**Are fasting tests done early in the morning?**
Yes, the lab opens at 7 am so fasting samples are convenient. For a fasting test, avoid eating for 8 to 12 hours; water and regular medicines are usually fine unless your doctor says otherwise.

**What should I bring for a lab test?**
Bring your prescription or the doctor's test list. If you are monitoring a condition like diabetes or thyroid, carrying your previous report helps compare results. No other preparation is needed for most routine tests.

**Is the laboratory open on Sundays?**
No, the lab is open Monday to Saturday, 7 am to 7 pm. For urgent needs on a Sunday, call the clinic and we will guide you on the best option.
