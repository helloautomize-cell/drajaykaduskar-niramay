**H1:** Niramay Pharmacy

Niramay Pharmacy (Niramay Medical) is located at the clinic, so you can collect your prescribed medicines right after your consultation.

**Phone:** +91 90213 51693
**Timings:** Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 8 pm. Sundays closed.
Our pharmacy is a licensed retail pharmacy. Licences are displayed at the pharmacy, and medicines are dispensed by a registered pharmacist.

#### Services
- Medicines dispensed against a valid prescription by a registered pharmacist
- **Home delivery in Nagpur**
- Insulin and other temperature-sensitive medicines stored in refrigeration and packed appropriately for transport
- [Diabetes](/diabetes/) supplies such as glucometers, strips and other everyday health products are available
- Help with understanding how and when to take your medicines

#### Important
- Prescription medicines are dispensed only against a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner.
- Always check the name, strength and expiry date when you receive your medicines.
- Do not change doses or stop medicines without talking to your doctor.
- Store insulin as instructed. Do not freeze it.

#### Frequently asked questions

**Do you deliver medicines?**
Yes, within Nagpur, and parcels can be arranged for outstation patients who take medicines regularly. Call the pharmacy with your prescription to confirm details.

**Do you stock diabetes and blood pressure medicines?**
Yes. As a clinic pharmacy we keep the medicines our doctors prescribe most, including diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid and cardiac medicines, along with common general medicines.

**Can someone else collect my medicines?**
Yes, a family member can collect with your prescription or a photo of it. If you need repeated monthly supplies, ask the pharmacist about parcel or delivery options.

**Do I need a Niramay prescription to buy medicines?**
No. We dispense against any valid prescription. Medicines that legally require a prescription are supplied only against one, whichever doctor has written it.

**Is the pharmacy open on Sundays?**
No, the pharmacy is open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 8 pm. If you are on regular medicines, we recommend keeping a few days' buffer so a Sunday does not interrupt a dose.
