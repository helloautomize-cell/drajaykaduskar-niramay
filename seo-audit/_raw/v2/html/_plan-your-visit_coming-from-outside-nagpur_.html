Coming from outside Nagpur?

Travelling from Wardha, Bhandara, Umred, Katol or nearby towns? Plan your visit so consultation, tests and medicines are done in one trip.

Medically reviewed by Dr. Ajay V. Kaduskar · Last reviewed 2 October 2026

Half our patients travel to us from towns and villages around Nagpur. This page helps you plan the trip so your consultation, tests and medicines are finished in one visit.

Planning your day

  • Book ahead by phone or WhatsApp so we can plan your visit.
  • If you need a fasting blood test, arrive by 7 am when the laboratory opens, do not eat before the sample, and carry your diabetes medicines to take with breakfast after the test.
  • The laboratory is open Monday to Saturday, 7 am to 7 pm; consultations are 8:30 am to 6 pm.
  • Most routine reports are ready the same day, so in most cases you can discuss results with the doctor before you travel back.

One visit, everything in one place

  • Consultation, blood and urine tests, ECG, 2D Echo, treadmill test and eye screening are all done at the clinic.
  • Pick up your medicines at our pharmacy right after your consultation, before you leave.

Follow-up without travel

Where clinically appropriate, your doctor may review stable follow-up reports by phone or WhatsApp, so you do not always need to travel for a routine review.

Getting here

Niramay Clinics is at 572, Indu Bhaskar Apartments, Dr. N. B. Khare Marg, opposite Dinanath High School, Dhantoli, about 3 km from Nagpur railway station and central bus stands. Two-wheeler parking is available at the building.

Patients regularly visit us from Kamptee, Hingna, Butibori, Kalmeshwar, Saoner, Katol, Narkhed, Umred, Bhiwapur, Kuhi, Mauda, Ramtek, Parseoni, Bhandara, Tumsar, Wardha, Hinganghat and Pandhurna.
