04 / Your first visit, step by step
Step 1 of 6

Book.

Call, WhatsApp or send a request. We confirm your time.

Step 2 of 6

Arrive and register.

Bring old reports and your medicines. Our staff check weight, waist and blood pressure.

Step 3 of 6

Consultation.

Your doctor takes a full history, examines you and explains what they find.

Step 4 of 6

Tests, if needed.

Blood tests, ECG, Echo or eye screening, mostly done the same day.

Targets, medicines and diet in writing. Collect medicines from our pharmacy if prescribed.

We book your next review so progress is tracked.
