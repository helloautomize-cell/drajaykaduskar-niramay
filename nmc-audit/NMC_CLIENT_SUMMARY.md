# Niramay Clinics: Doctor's Audit Summary

26 October 2026 | Investigation only | Live-site findings — no content was changed

## What this means for your practice

Niramay is a two-doctor outpatient clinic in Dhantoli, Nagpur, with a laboratory and pharmacy, not a hospital. The rules supplied for this audit favour factual information about doctors, services, timings and appointments, and balanced patient education rather than promotion.

The guideline is real and in force: we downloaded the official NMC public notice (No. R-13014/01/2024-Ethics, dated 6 October 2026, in force with immediate effect) from nmc.org.in and checked every page of your live website. This is a compliance review, not a legal opinion.

## What we found

**Overall risk: Severe.** There are **15 findings: 1 critical, 7 high and 7 medium**. The critical issue is identifiable people in existing clinic and retinal-screening photos; this is not a finding that a live data breach has been confirmed.

Both doctors' registration numbers are hidden pending confirmation. Several doctor images are explicitly documented as AI-created, including images used for identification. Portraits also appear repeatedly in booking-led promotional positions. The footer says the site is "Built & Marketed by" an agency.

Two clinical passages need prompt doctor review: blanket fasting instructions that could be read as postponing all insulin, and a claim that a yearly retinal photograph is the "only way" to detect diabetic eye damage. Other findings concern procedure risks, variable treatment/vaccine response, partly obscured patient photography, repeated article sales endings, an absolute programme promise and optional performance measurement.

The existing privacy policy, unticked form-consent boxes, educational disclaimers, factual practice experience and useful health information should be preserved.

## Changes proposed for a later, approved fix run

1. Remove identifiable patient/visitor imagery and retire the public copies; use existing anonymous equipment images only where appropriate.
2. Display both doctors' verified council registration details with their factual qualifications.
3. Remove AI doctor likenesses and promotional portrait repeats; keep at most one genuine identification photo on each profile.
4. Remove the marketing-agency claim and review the underlying services/payment terms. Relabelling a credit alone does not resolve prohibited marketing.
5. Correct the clinical passages after doctor approval, complete targeted risk/limitation wording, make article/contact language factual and resolve the measurement/privacy mismatch.

Consent or an AI label does not make prohibited promotional material acceptable under the supplied checklist. The detailed report provides replacement wording and preserves existing service URLs, educational answers and factual doctor attribution.

## What we need from you

- Each doctor's exact council number, current registration status and approved qualification/specialty wording.
- Genuine doctor-photo originals, image provenance and existing patient-image releases for internal review.
- Both doctors' approval of clinical corrections, risk statements, vaccination information and programme scope.
- The agency's actual service/payment arrangements and confirmation of data-retention/processor practices.
- Approval to proceed with the fix run described in the report.

No website code, content or images have been changed. Only audit deliverables are added. A later fix run and live verification are still required.
