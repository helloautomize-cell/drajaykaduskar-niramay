Prediabetes and Weight Loss: How Much Helps?

Dr. Ajay V. Kaduskar
,
Diabetologist
, Nagpur

MD (Medicine), PGDHSc (Diabetology), FEACD (The Netherlands)
Published

4 min read
Quick answer
Losing about 5 to 7 percent of your body weight, combined with regular physical activity, can significantly lower the risk of prediabetes progressing to type 2 diabetes. For someone weighing 80 kg, that means about 4 to 6 kg. Reducing waist size matters too, because fat around the abdomen is closely linked to insulin resistance.
Weight loss is one of the most effective ways to reverse prediabetes and prevent type 2 diabetes. You do not need to reach an ideal weight to benefit. Modest, steady weight loss can make a meaningful difference to how your body handles sugar.
Why does weight affect blood sugar?
Excess body fat, especially around the abdomen and inside the liver, makes cells less responsive to insulin. The pancreas then has to work harder to keep sugar in range. Over time, it may not keep up, and blood sugar rises. Losing weight reduces this fat and improves insulin sensitivity.
Why is waist size important?
Abdominal fat is more harmful than fat elsewhere. Many Indians have higher abdominal fat at a lower body weight. Waist circumference above about 90 cm in men and 80 cm in women is often used as a marker of higher risk in Asian populations. Reducing waist size improves metabolic health.
What is a realistic target?
Aim for 5 to 7 percent of body weight over several months
Lose weight gradually, often around 0.5 to 1 kg per week
Focus on sustainable habits rather than quick results
Maintain the loss over the long term
How can you lose weight safely?
Eat balanced meals
Fill half the plate with vegetables, add protein, choose whole grains and control portions of rice and rotis.
Cut liquid sugars
Avoid sugary drinks, juices and sweetened tea or coffee.
Increase activity
Aim for about 150 minutes of moderate activity per week plus strength training twice weekly.
Sleep and stress
Poor sleep and stress increase appetite and cravings.
Track progress
Weigh yourself weekly and measure your waist monthly.
Should you try crash diets?
Very low-calorie diets without supervision can cause nutrient deficiencies, muscle loss and weight regain. Some structured low-calorie programmes are used under medical supervision. Discuss options with your doctor.
What if weight loss is difficult?
Hormonal conditions such as hypothyroidism or PCOS, certain medicines and stress can make weight loss harder. Your doctor can check for these and may consider medicines that support weight loss in selected people.
A quick recap
Losing 5 to 7 percent of body weight lowers diabetes risk.
Abdominal fat is closely linked to insulin resistance.
Aim for gradual, sustainable weight loss.
Combine diet changes with regular activity.
Get medical help if weight loss is hard.
You do not need dramatic weight loss to protect yourself from diabetes. Small, steady changes add up. Dr. Ajay V. Kaduskar at Niramay Clinics in Nagpur supports patients with practical weight and prediabetes plans.
When to seek urgent care
Seek urgent care for extreme thirst with vomiting, confusion, deep rapid breathing or unusual drowsiness, which can signal very high sugar.

Frequently asked questions
How much weight should I lose to reverse prediabetes?
Losing about 5 to 7 percent of body weight can significantly reduce diabetes risk and often improves blood sugar. For an 80 kg person, this means about 4 to 6 kg. Your doctor can set a personal target.
Does losing belly fat help prediabetes?
Yes. Abdominal fat is strongly linked to insulin resistance. Reducing waist size through diet and exercise improves how the body uses insulin and lowers blood sugar.
How fast should I lose weight with prediabetes?
A gradual rate of about 0.5 to 1 kg per week is generally safe and easier to maintain. Rapid loss without supervision may cause muscle loss and weight regain.
Can I reverse prediabetes without losing weight?
Exercise and diet changes can improve blood sugar even with small weight changes, but weight loss usually gives the strongest benefit for people with excess weight.
What if I cannot lose weight despite trying?
Conditions such as thyroid problems, PCOS, stress or certain medicines can affect weight. Speak with your doctor, who may check for these and discuss additional support options.
