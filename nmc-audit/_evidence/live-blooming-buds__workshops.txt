Workshops for schools, parents and teachers
Workshops by Dr. Prajakta Kaduskar for schools, parents and teachers: puberty, mental wellness, screen use, cyber safety and exam stress.
Medically reviewed by
Dr. Prajakta A. Kaduskar
· Last reviewed
24 October 2026
Part of
Blooming Buds Child and Adolescent Care Centre
Some health topics are best discussed in groups: with a class of students, a parents' meeting or a teachers' staff room. Dr. Prajakta Kaduskar conducts interactive workshops that give accurate, age-appropriate information and practical skills.
Workshop topics
For students
Growing up: puberty and body changes (separate sessions for girls and boys where appropriate)
Menstrual health and hygiene
Managing exam stress and study habits
Healthy eating, sleep and physical activity
Digital detox and social media awareness
Anti-bullying and cyber safety
Life skills: decision-making, communication, handling peer pressure, emotional regulation
Substance use awareness: tobacco, vaping, alcohol and drugs
Self-esteem and confidence
For parents
Parenting adolescents: communication, boundaries and trust
Screens at home: setting family rules that work
Recognising stress, anxiety and low mood in children
Supporting children through exams and career decisions
For teachers
Recognising emotional and behavioural warning signs in students
Responding to bullying and cyberbullying
School health programmes and referral pathways
Format
Sessions can be held at your school or organisation in Nagpur. Workshops can be held in English, Hindi or Marathi. Call or use the form to plan one for your school or group. We will contact you within 2 working days.
Frequently asked questions
Who conducts the workshops?
Dr. Prajakta Kaduskar conducts them herself, sometimes with the clinic team. Sessions are planned with the school beforehand so the content fits the age group and the time available.
What topics can a school choose?
Common choices are puberty and growing up, mental wellness, screen and social media use, cyber safety, exam stress, study habits, bullying and life skills. A school can also request a custom topic.
How far in advance should we book?
Two to three weeks is usually comfortable so dates and content can be planned properly. Send the workshop request form or call the clinic and we will get back within two working days.
Is there a charge for workshops?
Charges depend on the session, the group size and the format. Schools can share their requirement through the request form or by phone and we will quote before confirming.
