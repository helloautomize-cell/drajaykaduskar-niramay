Psychological testing for children and teenagers
IQ, EQ, personality and aptitude assessment for children and teens in Nagpur, with a feedback session for parents.

Psychological tests help answer specific questions. Why is a bright child struggling at school? What are a teenager's strengths? How does a young person handle emotions and relationships? Results are most useful when they are explained carefully and turned into a practical plan.

Intelligence (IQ) testing:
understanding thinking and learning abilities, strengths and weaker areas

Good to know
A test score describes how a child performed on a particular day and is one piece of information, not a label.
Our reports help parents and schools plan support.
Official disability certificates are issued by government medical boards.
We can guide you on the process.
When to see a doctor
Consider an assessment when a child's learning, attention, behaviour or development concerns persist at school or at home - for example falling behind in reading despite effort, extreme restlessness, or regression after a stressful event. Testing is a way to understand, not to label.
Frequently asked questions
At what age can IQ be tested?
Standardised tests exist for young children through to adults. The right test is chosen by age and purpose.
Can my child prepare for the test?
No preparation is needed. Ensure a good night's sleep and a meal beforehand, and bring glasses or hearing aids if used.
Will my child be labelled after testing?
No. Testing is done to understand how your child learns and where support will help, not to label. Results are explained to parents in a feedback session with practical next steps.
How long does testing take?
Most assessments need one to two sessions depending on the tests chosen and the child's pace. We tell you the plan before starting so you can schedule the visits.
